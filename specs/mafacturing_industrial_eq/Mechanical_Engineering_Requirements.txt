MECHANICAL ENGINEERING REQUIREMENTS
Manufacturing and Industrial Equipment
Document ID: ME-REQ-001
Version: 1.1
Status: Generic project template — requirements require stakeholder validation

1. PURPOSE
This document defines stakeholder, functional, performance, safety, operational, maintenance, and verification requirements for manufacturing and industrial equipment. It is intended to support design planning, implementation, verification, and acceptance.

The requirements below are generic. Replace bracketed fields with project-specific values and confirm every requirement with the relevant stakeholders before approval.

2. REQUIREMENT WRITING RULES
- Each requirement has a unique ID.
- Use "shall" for a mandatory, verifiable requirement.
- Each requirement should describe one obligation and be testable by inspection, analysis, demonstration, or test.
- Record rationale, verification method, owner, and status where useful.

3. STAKEHOLDER AND BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS
BR-001 — The equipment shall perform the production or manufacturing process defined in the approved process description.
Verification: Design review and process demonstration.

BR-002 — The equipment shall meet the agreed production target of [units per hour] under the documented operating conditions.
Verification: Production trial using an agreed measurement period and counting method.

4. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
FR-001 — The equipment shall accept, locate, process, transfer, assemble, inspect, or otherwise handle the specified workpiece according to the approved process sequence.
Verification: Functional test.

FR-002 — The equipment shall provide the operating modes defined in the approved operating concept: [manual / setup / automatic / maintenance].
Verification: Demonstration of each applicable mode.

FR-003 — The equipment shall detect the specified process and equipment faults, and shall prevent automatic production from starting unless the required start and protective conditions are satisfied.
Verification: Fault-injection tests and functional safety validation.

FR-004 — The equipment shall provide a controlled method to recover from a detected fault without bypassing required protective measures.
Verification: Demonstration under documented fault scenarios.

5. PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS
PR-001 — The equipment shall achieve a cycle time no greater than [seconds] for the defined standard work cycle.
Verification: Timed test over an agreed number of cycles.

PR-002 — The equipment shall achieve positioning accuracy of [value and unit] and repeatability of [value and unit] under stated test conditions, if these functions apply.
Verification: Measurement using a suitable calibrated instrument.

6. MECHANICAL AND MATERIAL REQUIREMENTS
MR-001 — The frame and load-bearing components shall support all approved static, dynamic, impact, and foreseeable operating loads.
Verification: Structural analysis and inspection.

MR-002 — Wear components shall have defined inspection and replacement criteria.
Verification: Maintenance-document review.

7. SAFETY REQUIREMENTS
SR-001 — A documented risk assessment shall be completed and maintained throughout the design process, and identified risks shall be reduced through inherently safe design measures and safeguards.
Verification: Review of approved risk-assessment records.

SR-002 — Access to hazardous moving parts shall be prevented or controlled by guards and/or protective devices appropriate to the assessed risks.
Verification: Inspection and safety validation.

SR-003 — Emergency-stop devices shall be provided where required by the risk assessment, be accessible, and perform the defined stop function; resetting an emergency stop shall not by itself initiate a hazardous restart.
Verification: Inspection and functional test.

SR-004 — The equipment shall provide means to isolate and safely manage hazardous energy sources, including stored energy, during maintenance and servicing.
Verification: Inspection and maintenance-task validation.

8. ERGONOMIC AND HUMAN-FACTORS REQUIREMENTS
ER-001 — Normal operating controls shall be identifiable, accessible, and arranged to support the approved operating tasks.
Verification: Usability review and operator trial.

9. UTILITIES AND ENVIRONMENTAL REQUIREMENTS
UR-001 — The equipment shall operate within the approved site utility limits for electrical power, compressed air, hydraulics, vacuum, network, and other required services.
Verification: Utility review and commissioning test.

UR-002 — The equipment shall function within the specified ambient temperature and humidity range of [values].
Verification: Design review and, where necessary, environmental test.

10. MAINTENANCE AND RELIABILITY REQUIREMENTS
MA-001 — The supplier or design team shall provide a preventive-maintenance plan listing tasks, intervals, required skills, consumables, and tools.
Verification: Document review.

11. QUALITY AND ACCEPTANCE REQUIREMENTS
QR-001 — The project shall define and execute factory acceptance testing (FAT) and site acceptance testing (SAT), including pass/fail criteria and responsible signatories.
Verification: Approved and completed FAT/SAT records.

12. INSTALLATION REQUIREMENTS
IA-001 — The equipment shall be installed in accordance with approved layout, foundation, anchoring, utility, and clearance requirements.
Verification: Site inspection.

13. VERIFICATION AND TRACEABILITY
Each requirement above is tracked with its verification method, responsible owner, and approval status in the project's traceability matrix (not reproduced here).

14. ASSUMPTIONS AND OPEN QUESTIONS
Open items for this template include the exact process, workpiece range, production target, duty profile, site utilities, applicable safety standards, and FAT/SAT acceptance criteria — all to be confirmed with stakeholders before approval.
