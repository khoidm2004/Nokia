FleetLink Vehicle Telematics Platform
Requirements Specification, version 0.9 (draft)

1. Purpose

This document describes the requirements for FleetLink, a web based platform
that lets fleet operators monitor and manage commercial vehicles in real time.

2. Functional Requirements

2.1 Operator Sign-In
Fleet operators must sign in with an email address and a password before any
other part of the platform becomes available. A session expires after 30
minutes of inactivity.

2.2 Vehicle Registration
A signed-in fleet operator can register a vehicle by entering its licence
plate, its VIN and the identifier of the telematics device installed in it.
The same VIN cannot be registered twice.

2.3 Live Location Tracking
Once a vehicle is registered, the platform receives a GPS position from that
vehicle's telematics device every 10 seconds and shows the vehicle's current
location on a map.

2.4 Geofence Alerts
A fleet operator can draw a geofence around an area on the map. Whenever a
tracked vehicle crosses a geofence boundary, the platform raises a geofence
alert for that vehicle.

2.5 Maintenance Scheduling
The platform schedules a maintenance reminder for each registered vehicle
based on the odometer readings reported by that vehicle's telematics device.

2.6 Daily Trip Reports
At the end of each day the platform generates one trip report per vehicle
containing the distance travelled, the idle time and every geofence alert
raised for that vehicle during the day.

2.7 Offline Buffering Mode
If a telematics device loses network coverage it buffers its positions
locally and uploads them once the connection returns. Offline buffering mode
and live location tracking cannot both be active for the same vehicle: while
a device is buffering, the map shows only its last known position.

3. Non-functional Requirements

3.1 Map Refresh Latency
The live map must refresh within 2 seconds of the platform receiving a new
position.

3.2 Data Retention Policy
Raw GPS positions must be retained for 90 days and trip reports must be
retained for 7 years.
